name: Crypto bias calibration pitfall
description: Why the computeSignedBias() feedback loop was removed from Claude prompts and must not be re-added without model-source tracking.
---

## Rule
Do NOT inject `computeSignedBias()` output into Claude prompts unless the history records are confirmed to have been generated by Claude itself.

**Why:** The tracker now defaults to the statistical model. History records in `historyStore` are tagged as neither "claude" nor "stat" — they are mixed. When Claude runs on-demand via "Enhance", it receives a calibration instruction built from the stat model's errors. The stat model has a completely different bias pattern; applying its correction to Claude causes systematic directional flips (e.g. "shift DOWN 2%" crosses a Kalshi strike and inverts the ABOVE/BELOW call even when the market is clearly moving up).

**How to apply:** If bias calibration is ever re-introduced, `PredictionRecord` must gain a `source: "ai" | "stat"` field, and `computeSignedBias()` must filter to `source === "ai"` records only before computing the signed error. Until then, leave the function in place but do not call it from inside any prompt builder.

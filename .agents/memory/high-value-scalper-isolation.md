---
name: High-value scalper isolation
description: Safety boundary for the late-window Kalshi scalper and its relationship to the regular bot.
---

The high-value scalper must remain an independently owned execution system. It may coexist with a regular position on the same ticker, but it must never share normal-bot triggers, eligibility, reservations, orders, positions, settlement, quiet hours, metrics, or dashboard state.

**Why:** A previous combined implementation introduced duplicate and out-of-range live orders. Isolation makes the scaler’s caps, unknown-order halt, records, and incident response independently auditable, and prevents normal trading behavior from changing as a side effect.

Only outcomes that prove no exposure exists may re-arm the same durable symbol/window reservation. Transient quote, Freefall, and balance skips can re-arm after bounded cooldowns; confirmed zero-fill IOC orders can retry up to three total submissions. Any positive fill, submitting/unknown state, identity/risk/cap failure, or arbitrary error is terminal.

**Why:** A transient authenticated quote moving outside the band previously consumed the window forever, even when it returned to a valid late-window price. Re-arming only proven no-exposure outcomes restores that opportunity without weakening duplicate-order protection.

**How to apply:** Put new scalper behavior only in its dedicated modules, tables, routes, and panels. Before changing the feature, fingerprint the protected normal-bot execution files and verify they remain byte-for-byte unchanged. Preflight may warm identity, balance, cap headroom, and guard samples, but must never reserve funds or create/submit orders. Retry by atomically reclaiming the existing unique reservation, not by creating another row; count submitted zero fills durably across restarts. Bound background and authoritative data fetches with per-symbol deduplication and shared concurrency limits. Unknown live order outcomes retain worst-case reserved exposure and must block a circuit-breaker reset until an operator reconciles them with the exchange. Scalper mutations require a valid nonblank Clerk identity, matching the regular bot's signed-in-user policy; signed-out requests fail closed, with no separate operator secret or role system. Each attempt must size from its atomically reserved immutable risk snapshot; reject any risk/config change again after all pre-submit awaits and after persisting the live order intent. When Freefall Guard is enabled, missing, stale, invalid, or insufficient-lookback price data must block entry. Operator config patches must be strictly typed and allowlisted; never coerce strings or expose internal breaker fields. Dashboard unification is allowed only as a read-layer projection of confirmed Scalper fills; never write Scalper orders into regular tables or regular in-memory positions.

**Circuit-breaker control:** Breaker enforcement is an operator-owned switch that defaults on. Turning it off permits scans to continue but must still latch and persist every safety event and reason; re-enabling enforcement immediately blocks on any uncleared latch. Reset remains a separate action and still refuses unresolved live exposure. Display a server-generated plain-English explanation, never the machine reason code.

**Why:** Operators may intentionally accept continued trading after non-uncertain anomalies, but disabling a pause must not erase audit evidence. Concurrent settings saves previously could overwrite a trip, so every persistent config mutation must be serialized and a monotonic breaker-event version must make newer trips win before and after async writes. Failed trip persistence retries until durable.

**Unknown-order recovery:** Generate and durably save a unique client order ID before every live submission, then send that exact ID to Kalshi. Reconcile only from one uniquely matched authenticated exchange order plus complete fill evidence. Prefer exchange order ID, then client ID; legacy fallback requires exact ticker, side, initial count, YES limit price, a tight timestamp, and exclusion of known sibling IDs. Terminal IOC cancellation may retain an unfilled remainder, but filled plus remaining must exactly equal initial count. Preserve fractional fixed-point quantities and aggregate fill cost without intermediate rounding.

**Why:** One logical attempt can have multiple uncertain submission records, and Kalshi fixed-point quantities can be fractional. Guessing from a partial response, rounding VWAP-derived spend, or treating a canceled remainder as malformed can either release real exposure or leave a safe reservation stuck.

**How to apply:** Show one logical attempt to the operator while keeping a separate reconcile action for every unresolved order record. Resolving one sibling must keep the shared reservation held; release it only after the final sibling has authoritative zero-fill or fill evidence. Missing, duplicate, malformed, conflicting, incomplete, non-terminal, or failed lookup evidence remains unresolved.

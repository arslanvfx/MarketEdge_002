---
name: Scalper tail-risk entry floor
description: Why the High-Value Scalper target-distance minimum is a hard safety boundary.
---

High-Value Scalper entries must remain at least 0.20% from the Kalshi target. Persisted legacy values and operator edits below that floor must not weaken the runtime boundary.

**Why:** A 14-day production review found live entries below 0.15% were net negative because a few near-total losses overwhelmed many tiny wins. Entries at 0.20% or farther had no losses in that sample. A 97.9¢ XRP entry only 0.1432% from target lost $48.95.

**How to apply:** Treat 0.20% as the minimum target-distance safety floor for this Scalper lifecycle. Recalibrate only with comparable live data that includes tail losses, not headline win rate alone.

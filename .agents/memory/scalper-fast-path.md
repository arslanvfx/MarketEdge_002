name: Scalper final-minute fast path
description: Safety and telemetry constraints for keeping Scalper submission latency low near market close.
---

Serialize scans and coalesce overlap into one immediate follow-up pass instead of either overlapping work or dropping ticks. Begin non-submitting preflight 180 seconds before eligibility, using a lighter cadence early and a one-second cadence near entry. Continuously arm each exact market with an authenticated orderbook quote, exchange route, and authoritative guard sample. Candidate detection and final validation may consume only a current-window armed value no more than one second old; stale or changed preparation rejects the observation and waits for the next background refresh rather than launching candidate-time I/O. Of three underlying-price fetch lanes, background sampling may occupy at most two so authoritative preparation always has a lane. A started background fetch must never satisfy an authoritative request: use distinct in-flight ownership, tag sample provenance, and pin every execution guard to the newest authoritative boundary so a later cached completion cannot replace it.

**Why:** Final-minute candidates can leave the configured band while waiting behind a long scan, background sample backlog, or avoidable database round trips. Lower latency must not come from deleting safety checks or increasing uncontrolled concurrency. Promise coalescing alone is insufficient: a cache-eligible background job can race with a retry and contaminate a shared history unless provenance is preserved through guard evaluation.

**How to apply:** Keep exact identity, authenticated orderbook, fresh Freefall/target-distance evidence, routed funding, regular/Contrarian ownership, immutable risk revalidation, IOC cost ceiling, and strict unknown reconciliation. The live armed path performs all dynamic checks before one advisory-locked transaction that atomically claims cap/reservation capacity and inserts the submitting intent; after that await, re-read the newest armed state and synchronously validate identity, route, window, band, side, layering, proximity, and movement guards immediately before the broker call. Never await between a successful final validation and the submission call. If atomic activation succeeds but later local persistence fails, retain ownership fail-closed for reconciliation. Confirmed zero fills re-arm through a new prepared observation instead of immediately fetching/retrying inside the old attempt. Dashboard timing starts at authenticated-candidate detection and records prepared quote/sample age plus activation and broker timing.

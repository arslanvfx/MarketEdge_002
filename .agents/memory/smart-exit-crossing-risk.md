---
name: Smart Exit crossing-risk policy
description: Durable safety and calibration rules for deciding when a winning position may be exited early.
---

Smart Exit aggression scales from exact seconds remaining. A target crossing never authorizes an exit by itself. Underlying direction/distance and fresh held-side Kalshi deterioration must agree. A fresh authenticated full-position executable book is authoritative over slower public market probability. The guarded critical terminal-stop path may bypass the ordinary positive-sale-economics test only for an actual or imminent confirmed crossing with material authenticated-book deterioration.

The canonical bands are monitor above 240 seconds, escalation at 181–240, urgent at 61–180, and critical at 0–60. Minimum adverse excursions/latches are respectively 0.35%/4s, 0.20%/6s, 0.075%/8s, and 0.02%/10s. Critical may act on a sharp projected crossing before the target, but still requires independent underlying confirmation and fresh Kalshi corroboration.

Every executable signal still requires fresh underlying, quote, and order-book evidence; full-position liquidity; durable owner authorization; and final execution safeguards. Ordinary economic/value-preservation exits keep their positive-sale floor. A terminal stop uses the worst consumed price from full authenticated depth as its immutable floor. Final live submission re-runs policy and must preserve the exact typed authorization (`economic`, `value_preservation`, or `terminal_stop`) plus floor; authorization changes fail closed. PREPARE EXIT remains non-executing.

Live policy and durable replay use the same event-time trajectory, latch, recovery, time-band, crossing-risk, and value-preservation transitions. Duplicate spot events preserve a recent adverse latch without adding proof. Because the authenticated order-book API has no exchange event sequence, unchanged-book fingerprints retain their first observation time; repeated polls cannot fabricate fresh confirmation. Feed gaps reset crossing and low-price duration clocks. Genuine recovery, stale/out-of-order evidence, unhealthy spread, partial depth, or latch expiry clears executable pressure.

The hot ticker lane is isolated from slower full-evidence collection. Coinbase REST ticker `time` is a last-trade timestamp and may freeze for tens of seconds on thinner products, so the hot lane uses successful receipt time as observation time while exact duplicate prices remain non-distinct trajectory samples. Per-position evaluation is serialized/coalesced. Active regular and Scalper positions must be serviced before inactive prewarming and share the 500 ms hot lane. Slow spot, tape, and L2 inputs are reusable only when transport and exchange event times are fresh. Missing volatility, momentum, or book imbalance makes a crossing UNAVAILABLE. Trade flow may be optional only in the critical band or the fully confirmed final-three-minute value-preservation path; missing execution evidence always blocks.

Deep-loss protection is based on fresh full-position executable sale proceeds versus the actual remaining entry stake. A loss below 80% preserves ordinary policy, including terminal stops. From 80% to below 90%, an otherwise valid exit is blocked only when at least 210 seconds remain and recovery is reachable. At 90% or worse, an otherwise valid exit is always blocked. Scalper terminal exits use a separate authenticated full-depth 10¢ capital-preservation floor and must retain exact authorization through final revalidation.

Live authorization is per owner and symbol. Any applied version used for execution must carry the immutable parameter snapshot that replay validated; label-only legacy versions fail closed. Different symbols may be approved or rejected independently, but do not introduce a global target-cross delay from sparse outcomes.

Sensitivity is exposed only through three canonical presets so crossing thresholds cannot drift into invalid combinations. More Aggressive uses 2 confirmations, Default 3, and Less Aggressive 4 for the projected-crossing path; no preset may bypass monitor recovery protection, time-band distance, Kalshi confirmation, or execution safeguards. Applied live versions freeze both the preset name and all resolved values.

Cross-mode effectiveness comparisons must replay every sensitivity against one identical settled-position/evidence snapshot; explicit caller filters or limits cannot replace the canonical global snapshot. Confirmed fills, observed shadow simulations, and replayed counterfactuals are separate accounting classes. Missing liquidity coverage or quantity mismatch is unscoreable, never assumed executable.

**Why:** Early 15-minute crossings often recover, so ordinary safeguards remain strict. But a production XRP NO position crossed with about 47 seconds left while authenticated executable value collapsed from roughly 34¢ toward zero; stale public probability stayed near 87.5% and vetoed every exit. Terminal protection must trust executable depth before recoverable value disappears.

**How to apply:** Route live and replay through shared trajectory, market-pressure, time-band, crossing, and deep-loss assessments. Derive held-side deterioration from full authenticated executable depth when available, while retaining public quotes only as fallback telemetry. Require full quantity, fresh identity, and exact typed authorization at every final boundary. Never let terminal authorization weaken an economic/value-preservation floor, and never let a stale public quote veto a valid terminal stop.

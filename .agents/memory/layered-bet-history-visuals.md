---
name: Layered bet history visuals
description: Visual convention for regular bet cards and regular-plus-Scalper history groups.
---

Regular Kalshi bet-history cards use the same premium bento-card language as Scalper history, but with a deep navy and sky-blue treatment instead of luxury gold. When a Scalper fill layers onto a regular position, present both records as one hybrid unit: gold Scalper details with the linked regular bet nested in navy.

**Why:** The user wants regular and Scalper records to feel like one product family while remaining instantly distinguishable, and linked fills should read as one coordinated market action rather than unrelated duplicate cards.

**How to apply:** Preserve gold for Scalper, navy for regular bets, and use the durable regular-position link to group them. Keep outcome and P&L colors semantic inside both treatments.
